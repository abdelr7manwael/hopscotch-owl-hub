Hopscotch Owl Hub

A private tool used only by the owner of the Hopscotch Owl Books YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Uploads and schedules short videos that the owner made about their own children's activity books to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Reads the channel name to show which channel is connected before anything is uploaded.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It is not a public service, it has no sign-up, and it never accesses anyone else's account.
